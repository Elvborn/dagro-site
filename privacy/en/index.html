Privacy policy for Dagro

Effective from 20 November 2026

In short

Dagro is a to-do and routine app that works without the internet. Everything you write in the app stays on your phone. We collect no data about you.

  • No account and no login.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics, tracking or statistics.
  • We share and sell no data, because we receive none.
  • Dagro has no server. The app itself sends nothing over the internet.

What the app stores

Your to-dos, routines, what you have done or skipped, notes and settings are stored in a database on your phone. Only you and the app can reach it. We cannot see it.

Permissions and what they are for

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): for the reminders you set up yourself. The app only asks when it needs to.
  • Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): so a reminder arrives at the right minute. Without it, reminders still come, just a little less precisely.
  • After a restart (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): Android forgets alarms when the phone restarts. The app plans them again.
  • Calendar (READ_CALENDAR): only if you turn on "Show events from the calendar". The app reads the day's events from the calendars you choose and shows them. Events are not copied into the app and are not sent anywhere. The app never changes anything in your calendar. If you make a to-do from an event, or hide one, the app only remembers the event's id and start time.
  • Purchase (com.android.vending.BILLING): for the one-time purchase that unlocks the app. See "Purchase" below.
  • Internet and network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): added by Google's own libraries for purchases and ratings in Google Play. Dagro's own code does not use the internet.
  • Background work (WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): added by Android's library for background tasks, which the widget uses to stay up to date.

Backup

If backup is turned on on your phone, Android copies the app's database to your own Google account. That way your data can come along to a new phone. This is handled by Android and Google, not by us, and we have no access to the copy. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Export and deletion

  • Export data (Settings): saves all your data as a file in a place you choose. The file only leaves the phone if you send it on yourself.
  • Delete all data (Settings): removes all to-dos, routines, notes and settings from the phone.
  • Uninstall the app: the app's data is then deleted from the phone. Any backup in your Google account can be deleted in Google's settings.

You can always see, export and delete your data, also after the trial and without a purchase.

Purchase

The one-time purchase goes through Google Play. Google handles the payment and your payment details. We only learn that the app is unlocked. Google's libraries may send technical information to Google to complete the purchase. This is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Feedback and rating

  • Send feedback opens a form in your browser. The app version, Android version, phone model and language are filled in on the form, and you can clear them before sending. Nothing is sent until you press send yourself. The form is hosted by Google Forms.
  • Rate Dagro opens the app's page in the Play Store. Once, the app may also ask Google Play to show Google's own rating dialog. What you write there goes to Google.

The phone's assistant

On Android 16 and newer, the phone's assistant (such as Gemini) can add to-dos, read your day and mark things as done. The work itself happens on the phone. The assistant may send what you say, and the titles it receives, to its own servers. That falls under the assistant's own privacy policy. You can turn it off under Settings → Assistant.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update it here, along with the date at the top.
